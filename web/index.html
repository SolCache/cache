FAQ

Have any questions?

Still curious?

Ask the team on X. We answer everything.

How does Cache see my trades?

Solana transactions are public. Cache follows your wallet address and spots each swap you make, whatever app you use. It only reads; it can't touch a trade or slow it down.

Does Cache hold my money?

No. Your USDC stays in your wallet until a weekly sweep, and the xStocks it buys go straight back to your wallet in the same transaction.

What does the approval let Cache do?

It lets Cache spend USDC up to the weekly limit you set, and only to buy the xStocks you picked, delivered to your own wallet. It can't send funds anywhere else.

What are xStocks?

xStocks are tokens issued by a third party that track the price of real stocks and ETFs, like Tesla or the S&P 500. They aren't available in every country, including the United States, so check that you're eligible before you start.

How do I pause or stop?

Pause round-ups in the app anytime. To stop for good, revoke the approval in Cache or directly in your wallet.

What does it cost?

Nothing. Cache is directly supported by $CACHE on pump.fun. Network fees for each sweep are covered by Cache and are usually a fraction of a cent. Maintenance is funded directly through creator rewards.
