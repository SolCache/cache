How Cache works.

What happens to your round-ups, exactly what Cache can and can't touch in your wallet, and where to check every piece of it yourself. All of the code is public.

Overview

Cache watches the swaps your Solana wallet makes on any app, rounds each one up to the next dollar, and once a day buys the tokenized stocks (xStocks) you picked with that spare change. What it buys lands straight in your own wallet.

  1. Connect and choosePick up to 5 xStocks, a weekly limit, a multiplier, and whether to fund round-ups with USDC or SOL.
  2. Approve onceOne signature gives Cache's onchain program permission to spend from your funding account, up to your weekly limit. Nothing leaves your wallet at this point.
  3. Trade like you always doCache sees each swap your wallet signs and adds its round-up to your pending total.
  4. Daily sweepOnce a day the pending total is swapped through Jupiter into your picks and sent to your wallet.

Round-ups

Each swap is valued in US dollars and rounded up to the next whole dollar. A swap that's already a whole dollar adds a full $1. Your multiplier (1x, 2x, 5x, 10x, 20x, 50x or 100x) then scales the round-up.

  • Only transactions your own wallet signed and paid the fee for count. Incoming transfers and airdrops don't.
  • Swaps that buy or sell xStocks are skipped, so Cache's own purchases and your stock sales never trigger more round-ups.
  • Round-ups start from the moment you set Cache up. Earlier history isn't counted.

Sweeps

Cache checks for new swaps every 15 minutes and sweeps once a day at 12:00 UTC. You can also press Sweep now in the app.

  • A sweep only runs once at least $1 is pending, so tiny amounts aren't eaten by network fees.
  • The pending amount is split evenly across your picks. When it's too small to split (under $0.50 a pick), it all goes to one pick, rotating each sweep. Each pick is its own swap through Jupiter, with at most 1% slippage.
  • Each sweep is capped by your weekly limit (up to $100,000, or 1,000 SOL when funding with SOL). The limit resets 7 days after it last started and is enforced by the program onchain, not just by the website.
  • Every sweep is a public transaction. Your dashboard links each one to a block explorer.

Funding: USDC or SOL

USDC

Your USDC stays in your normal USDC account. During setup you approve Cache's vault (a program address unique to your wallet) as a delegate on that account, for a set amount. Cache can only spend within that approval and your weekly limit, and you can top up or revoke it at any time.

SOL

You set aside an amount of SOL into a Cache SOL account. It's a wrapped-SOL token account that you own, kept separate from your main balance so wallets don't sweep it up. Cache's vault is approved as delegate on that account only. Your main SOL balance is never touched.

You can add more SOL, or withdraw part or all of it back to your main balance, from the dashboard at any time.

What Cache can touch

Sweeps are signed by a server wallet called the keeper. The keeper never holds your funds and can't send them anywhere you didn't choose. The Cache program checks every sweep onchain and rejects the whole transaction if any check fails:

Only your funding account

Spending comes only from the USDC or Cache SOL account you approved, never your other tokens or your main SOL.

Only up to your limit

Each sweep counts against your weekly limit, tracked onchain in your vault.

Only to your wallet

The output has to land in a token account owned by you, for a stock in your picks and on the program's allowlist.

Only a real swap in between

A sweep opens and closes inside one transaction. The only instructions allowed in between are Jupiter's swap program, compute budget and token-account creation.

A minimum output

Each swap has to deliver at least a quoted minimum amount of stock, or it fails.

Leftovers come back

Anything the route didn't use is refunded to your funding account in the same transaction. The keeper ends with nothing extra.

Revoking the approval, from the app or from your wallet, stops Cache immediately. Your stocks are always in your own wallet and Cache has no way to move them.

Pausing, withdrawing, stopping

  • Pause stops sweeps without changing anything else. Resume whenever you like.
  • Edit settings changes your picks, weekly limit or multiplier with one signature.
  • Withdraw SOL moves 25%, 50% or all of your Cache SOL back to your main balance.
  • Convert all to USDC sells the xStocks in your wallet back to USDC. You review a quote and sign it yourself.
  • Stop Cache revokes the approval, closes your vault and returns its rent to you. With SOL funding, it also closes the Cache SOL account and returns the SOL to your main balance.

Fees

Cache charges no fee during the test. The keeper pays the network fees for sweeps. You pay the normal network fee on the transactions you sign yourself (setup, edits, withdrawals), and swaps carry the usual pool fees and price impact of the route Jupiter picks.

Source code

Cache is open source. The program, the website, the app and the keeper all live in one public repository, and every deploy is built from it by GitHub Actions. The repository's build also runs the program against a local Solana validator before anything ships.
