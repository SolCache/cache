Terms of Use

What Cache is

Cache is software. It reads the public activity of a wallet you connect, calculates round-ups, and, within the limits you approve onchain, swaps those round-ups into tokenized stocks that are delivered to your own wallet. Cache never takes custody of your funds or your keys. Every transaction that changes your settings is signed by you, in your wallet.

  • Making sure you're eligible to use Cache and to hold the assets it buys where you live.
  • Any taxes that apply to your trades, round-ups and holdings.
  • Reviewing every transaction your wallet asks you to sign.

Fees

Cache doesn't charge a fee for round-ups or sweeps. Swaps carry the usual pool fees and price impact of the route used, and transactions you sign yourself carry the normal Solana network fee.

Privacy Policy

Cache has no accounts. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number, and we don't sell data.

What Cache uses

  • Your public wallet address, to find your swaps and run your sweeps.
  • Public blockchain data for that address: your swaps, their values and the round-ups calculated from them. This is stored so your dashboard and sweeps work.
  • Your Cache settings, which live onchain in your vault, like anything else on Solana.

Services that see some data

  • Our hosting provider processes standard request data, such as your IP address and browser type, to serve and protect the site.
  • Blockchain data providers and Jupiter receive your wallet address when Cache looks up your activity or prepares a swap.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

On your device

The app remembers which wallet you last connected, and the site remembers that you've seen the intro, using your browser's storage. Cache doesn't use tracking or advertising cookies.

Your choices

Disconnect your wallet or stop Cache whenever you like. Data that's already on the Solana blockchain is public and can't be deleted by anyone.

Risk Disclosure

Using Cache involves real risk, including the loss of everything you put in. Only use amounts you can afford to lose.

  • Market risk. Stock prices and crypto prices go up and down. Past performance doesn't predict future results, and projections on this site are illustrative only.
  • Tokenized stock risk. xStocks are issued by a third party and track the price of an underlying share or fund. They may not carry the same rights as the underlying asset, such as voting rights, and depend on the issuer and its custodians. Their price can differ from the underlying, especially when markets are closed or liquidity is thin.
  • Smart contract and software risk. The Cache program, the app and the services they rely on may contain bugs or vulnerabilities, or behave in ways we don't expect.
  • Approval risk. Cache works by you approving its vault to spend from one funding account, up to an amount you choose. The program limits how that approval can be used, but an approval is still permission to spend. Revoke it whenever you stop using Cache.
  • Execution risk. Swaps are subject to slippage, price impact and route availability. Transactions can be delayed or dropped when the network is busy.
  • Network risk. Solana and the services Cache uses can slow down, halt or change.
  • Regulatory risk. Rules for crypto and tokenized securities differ by country and can change, which may affect your ability to use Cache or hold the assets it buys.
